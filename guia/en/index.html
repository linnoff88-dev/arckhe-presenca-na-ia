How it works, in plain language

The five parts of the service

🪪 Registration

It's the business's ID card in AI. You search for the business in the Business Center and create a profile, or claim one that already exists ("claim" means asking to be recognized as the owner of a profile ChatGPT created on its own).

🔐 Verification

It's like having a document notarized. A line in the "DNS" (the control panel where the website address is set up) proves that the client owns the website. Then the owner confirms their identity.

📋 Business profile

Name, website, category, and a description of up to 300 characters, kept the same on the website, Google, and social media. Consistent information everywhere makes things clearer for AI.

🌐 Several AI tools

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity read the internet in different ways. You check all four, not just one.

📈 Monitoring

Every month, run the same diagnostic to compare with the previous month. When the Business Center dashboard (mentions and clicks) becomes available to the client, it goes into the report. The dashboard only opens after identity verification, and we have not seen it yet.

🚫 What it does not promise

The Business Center is in beta, and registering does not guarantee a recommendation or the top spot. You sell organization and monitoring.

Common questions

Before you sell

Do I need to code?

No. For the diagnostic, just ask questions and take notes in a browser. The DNS record goes in the control panel of the company that sold the domain (Registro.br, Hostinger, GoDaddy, Cloudflare…); the companion kit shows you exactly where.

How much should I charge?

The kit does not make up a market price. It gives you a calculation: estimated hours × your hourly rate, with room for surprises, plus a monthly fee for monitoring.

Can I guarantee the business will be recommended?

No. The Business Center is in beta, and OpenAI does not promise a position. Guarantee what you deliver: an official profile, verification in progress, correct details, and a report.

Do I need the client's password?

No. Ask for editor access to the DNS panel or work together on the client's screen. The owner always confirms their own identity.

Is this only for ChatGPT?

The official registration is with ChatGPT. The diagnostic and consistent profile details also help with Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity.

Does it cost anything?

The kit and registration are free. An agent (Claude Code or Codex) is optional and requires a separate paid plan.
